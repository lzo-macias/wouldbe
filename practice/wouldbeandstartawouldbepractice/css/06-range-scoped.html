CSS 06 — Restyle the goal slider (scoped so it can't leak)

Native range inputs look OS-default until you opt out with appearance: none, then paint the track (the input itself) and the knob (the ::-webkit-slider-thumb / ::-moz-range-thumb pseudo-elements). Match Target: a thin rounded gray track and a 22px gold knob with a soft ring. Crucially, scope every selector under .yours — a bare input[type=range] rule is global and would restyle every slider in the whole app (this is exactly why StartAWouldBe scopes its slider under its root class).
