This Privacy Policy explains what information CoolPeople collects, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. It is incorporated into our Terms of Service. Capitalized terms not defined here have the meaning given in the Terms.

1. A Note on Money & Political Contributions

Understanding our money model helps explain our data practices: CoolPeople never touches, holds, or processes campaign contributions. Campaign “pledges” are non-binding promises and never transact on the Service. The only real money we handle is (a) optional tips to the platform, (b) a flat fee paid by a debate poster/sponsor, and (c) a small per-transaction markup on debate-side transactions. Card payments are handled by our payment processor; we do not store full card numbers. Because we are not a contribution processor, we do not collect or retain donation/contribution payment data.

3. How We Handle Your Address (Transient-Only)

When you provide a street address, we send it to a geocoding provider to determine your civic jurisdictions. We retain only the derived jurisdiction links (and, where applicable, a coarse coordinate/centroid used for boundary resolution). We do not persist your street address. This minimizes sensitive location data while still letting us show you relevant offices, deadlines, and notifications.

4. How We Use Information

  • Provide, operate, secure, and improve the Service and its features (WouldBe campaigns, pledges, debates, posts).
  • Verify eligibility and age; record attestations and consents for legal compliance.
  • Resolve jurisdictions to match you with relevant offices, deadlines, candidates, and notifications.
  • Process the limited payments described in Section 1 and handle tax/reporting obligations for payouts.
  • Send communications you have consented to, and operate the candidate-notification system under pre-established, uniformly applied objective criteria (see Section 6).
  • Moderate content, enforce our Terms, prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with law and lawful requests.

5. Legal Bases (GDPR/UK GDPR, where applicable)

We process personal data on the bases of: contract (to provide the Service you request); consent (e.g., certain communications, optional data uses, and processing of special-category political-opinion data you choose to share); legal obligation (recordkeeping, tax, election and child-safety law); and legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, and improving the Service), balanced against your rights. You may withdraw consent at any time.

6. Political Activity & the Candidate Notifier

  • Information about your political activity (e.g., pledges, candidacy) is treated as sensitive. We do not sell individual political data (see Section 9).
  • Where we notify users about relevant WouldBe campaigns, we apply pre-established objective criteria, uniformly (consistent with applicable election-law guidance such as 11 CFR 110.13), personalized only on preferences you have stated, and we log the objective inputs and the criteria version for each send.
  • You control whether you receive these messages and on which channel, and can opt out at any time.

7. Communications & Consent (TCPA)

We send transactional and, where you opt in, promotional or notification messages by in-app, email, push, and/or SMS. We capture your consent with technical metadata. You can withdraw consent or unsubscribe at any time (e.g., reply STOP to SMS, use the unsubscribe link, or adjust settings). Withdrawing consent is recorded as a new record; we do not delete the prior consent history.

8. How We Share Information

We do not sell individual political data. We share information only as follows:

  • Service providers / processors acting on our behalf under contract — for example: payment processing ([Stripe]), media storage/delivery ([Cloudflare R2 / Stream]), geocoding ([geocodio]), analytics ([PostHog]), messaging, and livestreaming ([Twitch]).
  • Public-by-design content — your public profile, WouldBe pages, posts, debate entries, and similar content are visible to others as part of how the Service works.
  • Legal & safety — to comply with law, lawful requests, or to protect rights and safety; including mandatory reporting of child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and law enforcement as required by 18 U.S.C. § 2258A.
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, subject to this Policy.

9. “Sale” / “Sharing” of Personal Information (CCPA/CPRA)

We do not sell or share individual political data. To the extent any data practice is deemed a “sale” or “sharing” under California law, it is opt-in only and offered only to eligible users; pre-existing users are not enrolled. You may opt out (or decline to opt in) at any time, and we honor recognized opt-out preference signals where required. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of users we know to be under 16 without the required opt-in.

10. Data Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes described here or as required by law (including election, tax, and safety recordkeeping). Append-only records (attestations, consents, certain audit and moderation logs) are retained for their legal recordkeeping purpose even after related data changes. We operate retention policies that purge categories of data on defined schedules, and certain safety materials (e.g., CSAM-related preservation) are retained for legally mandated periods.
